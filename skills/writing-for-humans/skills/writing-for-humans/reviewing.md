# Review rounds (Deep level)

Run after the Standard steps, on a draft that is already scanner-clean. Up to three rounds. Each round dispatches three fresh subagents in parallel, then the author revises.

## The three reviewers

Give each one the draft, the reader line and the word ceiling, and nothing about how the piece was made.

1. **Cold reader.** Reads as the named reader. Reports, in under 100 words: what it thinks it is being asked to do and by when; any term it did not know; any claim that sounded surer than the draft supports; anything it would need to know before answering that the draft doesn't say.
2. **Voice match.** Also gets the two or three samples the author used. Points to the sentences that would not appear in those samples, and says what the user would have written instead. Only for pieces sent as the user.
3. **Cutter.** Also gets the triage list. Returns a shorter version under the ceiling. It may not remove any load-bearing qualifier on the triage list, and it may not add anything.

Brief every reviewer to cut before it adds. Reviewers asked for improvements default to adding caveats and explanation; that made earlier drafts longer and worse.

## Revising

Take the cutter's version as the base. Apply the cold reader's points where they show the reader would act differently or get stuck. Apply the voice points. Re-run the scanner.

Stop when a round produces nothing that would change what the reader does or how they would hear it. Most pieces stop after one round. Never run a fourth.

## Hand-over

Say how many rounds ran, then the left-out list. If the user has time, offer them the first draft and the final one side by side, unlabelled, and ask which they would send.
